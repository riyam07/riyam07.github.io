What do you know about black holes?

According to NASA, a black hole is "an astronomical object with a gravitational pull so strong that nothing, not even light, can escape it", but what else do we know about these mysterious objects in the universe? These giants not only destroy the objects around them, but also bend space and time itself, challenging our understandings of physics which makes them one of the most interesting objects we know of in astronomy.

Take a look at a few famous black holes below!

Additional Fun Astronomy Facts! ✧

  • 1 tsp of neutron star weighs the same as the human population!
  • A day on Venus is longer than its year.
  • Black holes have theoretical opposites known as white holes (my favorite fact, super interesting!). They are theorized to spit out matter and light instead of trapping it like a black hole.
  • Planets can wander around space without a parent star (often called rogue planets).
  • Saturn could theoretically float on water due to its density of 0.7 grams per cubic centimeter.
